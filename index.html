Our Story

SLEEPLUX is built on the experience, dedication and craftsmanship of our family.

Our founder, Shafiq Pinjari, comes from a middle-class family. With determination and hard work, he started his own mattress-making business and built his skills through years of hands-on experience.

With 25 years of experience in mattress making, Shafiq Pinjari continues to focus on quality, comfort and careful craftsmanship.

His son, Shoaib Pinjari, also works alongside him in the business. To help grow the family business and bring its products to more customers, he started the SLEEPLUX website.

Together, they continue the tradition of handcrafted cotton mattresses.
