The Story Behind SLEEPLUX

A Family Craft, A New Vision

The story of SLEEPLUX begins with my father, Shafiq Pinjari, who brings 25 years of experience in mattress making. Through his hard work, dedication, and craftsmanship, he built this business from the ground up.

Growing up, I watched my father work tirelessly and gradually understood the true value of his craft and the potential behind his business.

I wanted my father's traditional business to go beyond its existing identity and grow into something bigger — a modern brand with a distinct identity and a vision for the future. That vision inspired me to create SLEEPLUX.

As the founder's son, my goal is to take my father's years of experience and craftsmanship to the next level by combining traditional skills with a modern brand identity.

To me, SLEEPLUX is more than just a brand. It represents my father's years of hard work, my personal dream, and a legacy that we are building together.

Built on my father's experience. Driven by my vision.
